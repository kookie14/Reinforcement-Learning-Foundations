Why this blog exists

Textbooks on reinforcement learning are rigorous but dense. This series reads one of them carefully and rewrites the ideas for anyone who's curious.

Each post follows the same path: the problem, then the idea in plain words, then the equation, with every symbol explained, and finally a small example you can check by hand. Proofs only appear when they help the intuition.

The posts follow a learning path, not the book's chapter order. Two running examples come back again and again: a coffee shop that has to order milk, and a small grid world like the one on the home page.

Source: Reinforcement Learning: Foundations by Shie Mannor, Yishay Mansour and Aviv Tamar (October 2025 draft).
